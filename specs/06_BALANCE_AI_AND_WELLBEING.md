# MINDSET OS — SPECIFICATION 06: MINDSET BALANCE AI, FREE-TIME ENGINE & WELLBEING ANALYTICS
**Company:** LKS PVT LTD  
**Module:** MindSet Balance AI  
**Core Principle:** "Productivity is not about filling every minute. It is about using time meaningfully while leaving room to live."

## 1. Nine-Dimension Balanced Daily Scheduling (`packages/balance-engine`)
Every AI-generated daily schedule must balance 9 pillars without filling 100% of the clock:
1. Essential responsibilities (School/Work `08:00–17:00`)
2. Important goals (e.g., 590/600 Board Exam preparation)
3. Learning & personal development
4. Physical activity & outdoor movement
5. Mental relaxation & mindfulness
6. Intentional entertainment
7. Social & family time (`19:30` dinner/family block)
8. Unplanned flexible free time (mandatory daily buffer)
9. Sleep & recovery (`22:00–05:30` protected window)

## 2. Free-Time Detection & Energy/Mood Matrix
1. **Automatic Gap Detector:** Scans the daily timetable to identify open windows between commitments (e.g., a 45-minute or 60-minute gap between finishing study at `17:00` and the next activity at `18:00`).
2. **Four Energy Tiers:**
   - `HIGH`: Exercise, creative projects, outdoor sports, learning new skills.
   - `MEDIUM`: Reading, walking, photography, casual family conversation.
   - `LOW`: Relaxing music, quiet rest, light stretching, watching a selected movie/episode.
   - `VERY_LOW`: Screen-free rest, nap/recovery, reducing non-essential commitments.
3. **Nine Optional Mood States (`/mood`):**
   - `Happy`, `Calm`, `Excited`, `Bored`, `Tired`, `Stressed`, `Unmotivated`, `Curious`, `Creative`.
4. **Seven Relaxation Activity Categories:**
   - *A. Mental Relaxation* (meditation, breathing, gratitude writing)
   - *B. Physical Relaxation* (walking, stretching, cycling)
   - *C. Creative Activities* (drawing, music, building small projects)
   - *D. Intellectual Entertainment* (science documentaries, puzzles, astronomy/history)
   - *E. Social Activities* (talking with family, meeting friends)
   - *F. Intentional Entertainment* (planned movie, time-budgeted gaming, comedy)
   - *G. Complete Rest* (doing nothing without screens or guilt)

## 3. Intentional Entertainment vs. Unintentional Consumption
- **Handshake with Focus Guard:** When a user starts an approved *Intentional Entertainment* block or *Smart Free-Time Plan*, Balance AI automatically instructs Focus Guard to unlock the chosen game launcher or media player for that exact window—distinguishing planned enjoyment from compulsive scrolling.

## 4. Personal Hobby Discovery & Weekly Life-Balance Analytics (`/insights`)
1. **12-Category Hobby Discovery Engine:** Recommends and tracks beginner-friendly activities across *Art & Design, Technology, Music, Sports, Reading, Photography, Gardening, Cooking, Writing, Outdoor Exploration, Science,* and *DIY Projects* with anti-repetition rotation.
2. **Non-Competitive Recharts Analytics (`/insights`):**
   - Visualises weekly hours across all 9 life balance dimensions.
   - Generates supportive text insights without toxic productivity scores or competitive leaderboards.
