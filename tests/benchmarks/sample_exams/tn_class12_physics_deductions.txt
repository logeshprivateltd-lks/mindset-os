--- Question 1: Gauss's Law & Electric Flux ---
Statement:
Total flux through closed surface is q / epsilon_0.
Formula:
  phi_E = oint E . dS = q / epsilon_0

Diagram:
[DIAGRAM: Spherical Gaussian surface of radius r around charge q, with radial E vector and dS surface element arrows.]

Derivation:
  phi_E = E * 4 * pi * r^2
  E = q / (4 * pi * epsilon_0 * r^2)
  phi_E = q / epsilon_0

Final Result:
  Total Electric Flux phi_E = 1.13 * 10^5


--- Question 2: Electric Dipole in Uniform Electric Field ---
Torque on dipole:
  tau = p * E * sin(theta)
In vector form:
  tau = p x E
Special cases:
  theta = 0: tau = 0 (stable)
  theta = 90: tau = pE (max)
  theta = 180: tau = 0 (unstable)


--- Question 3: Wheatstone's Bridge Balance Condition ---
Statement: KCL and KVL rules applied.

Diagram:
[DIAGRAM: Four resistors P, Q, R, S in diamond network with galvanometer G.]

Derivation:
Applying loop laws:
  I1*P = I2*R
  I3*Q = I4*S
At null deflection Ig = 0, so I1 = I3 and I2 = I4.
Dividing:
  P / Q = R / S

Final Result:
  P / Q = R / S


--- Question 4: Biot-Savart Law on Circular Current Loop ---
Biot-Savart Law:
  dB = (mu_0 / 4pi) * (I dl sin(90)) / R^2

Diagram:
[DIAGRAM: Circular coil of radius R carrying steady current I. Point P along coil axis with distance x and radius R.]

Integrating along loop:
  B = (mu_0 * I / (4 * pi * R^2)) * (2 * pi * R)
  B = (mu_0 * I) / (2 * R)

Final Answer:
  B = 6.28 * 10^-5 Tesla (T)
